# Summary

Synchronization applies desired resources under an explicit policy. Automated sync, pruning and self-healing govern different triggers and consequences rather than one interchangeable automation switch.

Ordering and hooks can coordinate delivery but do not create a universal transaction. Recovery must align with source ownership and retained data, while failed sync, unhealthy resources and live drift need distinct diagnosis.
